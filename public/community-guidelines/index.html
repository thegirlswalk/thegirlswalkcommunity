How we look after each other

Community Guidelines

TGW is a space for women to walk, move and grow together. These guidelines apply in the TGW Community app, in our WhatsApp groups, and at every walk and event.

The short version: be kind, be safe, and keep it real. If something doesn't feel right, tell us.

Be kind

  • Cheer each other on. Every step counts, whether it's 2,000 or 20,000.
  • No harassment, bullying, hate speech, or put-downs about anyone's body, background, beliefs or pace.
  • Disagree respectfully. Keep conversations about ideas, not people.

Be safe

  • Meet in public places only. Official walks and approved tag-alongs always happen in public.
  • Don't share anyone's personal details (phone number, address, photos) without their permission.
  • Never ask members for money, and never send money to someone you've met through TGW.
  • Hosts of tag-alongs must be verified and approved by a TGW admin.

Keep it real

  • Use your real first name and a real photo of yourself.
  • Only real steps count. Don't add steps manually or use tricks to boost challenge totals.
  • No spam. Promotions, links and business posts go in the Marketplace, not in general chats.
  • No sexual, violent or illegal content.

Respect privacy

  • Ask before posting photos that show other members.
  • What's shared in a circle stays in that circle.

Reporting

Use Report on any profile, post or message in the app, or email info@thegirlswalkcommunity.com with the subject "Safety". A TGW admin reviews every report. You can also block someone; they won't be told, and you won't see each other's content. If you are in immediate danger, call 911.

What happens if guidelines are broken

Depending on what happened, we may remove content, give a warning, pause an account, or remove someone from the community, in the app and at events. Serious safety issues may be reported to the police.
