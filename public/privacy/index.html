Privacy Policy

Effective October 27, 2026

This policy explains how TheGWalk Community ("TGW", "we", "us") collects, uses and protects personal information when you use the TGW Community mobile app (the "App") and the website thegirlswalkcommunity.com (the "Site"). We are based in Toronto, Ontario, Canada, and we handle personal information in line with Canada's Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA).

The short version: we only collect what we need to run the community. We never sell your information, we never use your health or step data for advertising, and you can delete your account at any time.

1. Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Account details: your first name, last initial, email address and, if you choose, a profile photo and city.
  • Sign-in details: if you sign in with Apple or Google, we receive your name and email address (or Apple's private relay email) from that provider. We do not receive your Apple or Google password.
  • Community activity: events you RSVP to, challenges you join, cheers you send and receive, your settings, and any reports you submit.
  • Messages to us: anything you send when you contact support or request account deletion.
  • Website forms: your email address if you subscribe to our newsletter.

Step data (with your permission)

If you allow it, the App reads your daily step count from Apple Health (HealthKit) on iPhone or Health Connect on Android. We only read step counts. We do not read or write any other health data, and the App never writes data to Apple Health or Health Connect. You can turn this off at any time in your phone's settings.

Information collected automatically

  • Notification token: a device identifier used to send you push notifications if you allow them.
  • Technical information: app version, device type, operating system version and error logs, used to keep the App working and fix problems.

We do not use advertising identifiers, we do not show ads, and we do not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

2. How we use your information

  • To create and secure your account and verify that members are who they say they are.
  • To show events, send reminders and record your RSVPs.
  • To run step challenges: show your progress, streaks and, depending on your visibility setting, your step totals to your circles or the community.
  • To send notifications you have turned on, such as event reminders, cheers and the weekly Sunday note.
  • To keep the community safe, including reviewing reports and enforcing our community guidelines.
  • To respond to your questions and requests.
  • To produce anonymous, combined statistics (for example, total community steps or number of members) for grant applications and partners. These never identify you.

How we treat step data

  • Step data is used only to provide step tracking and challenges inside the App.
  • It is never sold, never used for advertising or marketing, and never used to make decisions about credit, insurance or employment.
  • It is never shared with third parties, except the service providers listed below who store it on our behalf.
  • By default, only members of your circles can see your step totals. You can change this in Me → Step visibility.

Our use of information received from Health Connect follows the Health Connect Permissions policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

3. Who we share information with

We do not sell or rent personal information. We share it only with:

  • Other members, as part of how the community works: your first name, last initial, profile photo, events you are going to, cheers, and step totals according to your visibility setting.
  • Service providers that run parts of the App and Site for us, under agreements that require them to protect your information:
    • Supabase (database, sign-in and file storage)
    • Expo (app updates and push notification delivery)
    • Apple and Google (sign-in and delivering notifications to your device)
    • Vercel (website hosting)
    • Our donation and email newsletter providers, if you use those services
  • Authorities, if required by law or to protect someone's safety.

4. Where your information is stored

Some of our service providers store information outside Canada, including in the United States. When that happens, your information is subject to the laws of that country, and may be accessed by its courts or authorities.

5. How long we keep it

  • We keep your account information for as long as your account is open.
  • When you delete your account, we delete your profile, step data, RSVPs, cheers and settings within 30 days. Copies in encrypted backups are removed within a further 60 days.
  • We may keep a limited record of safety reports or of donations where the law requires it, or to protect the community. Those records are kept only as long as necessary.

See how to delete your account.

6. Your choices and rights

  • Access and correction: you can see and edit your profile in the App, or ask us for a copy of the information we hold about you.
  • Step data: you can stop sharing at any time in Apple Health or Health Connect settings, and change who sees your steps in the App.
  • Notifications: you can turn them off in the App or in your phone's settings.
  • Withdraw consent and delete: you can delete your account in the App or on our website.
  • Complaints: contact us first. You also have the right to complain to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada.

7. Security

We use encryption in transit, access controls and the security features of our providers to protect your information. Only TGW admins who need access can view member records. No system is completely secure, so please use a strong sign-in method and tell us right away if you think your account has been compromised.

8. Age requirement

The App is for adults aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If you believe a minor has created an account, please contact us and we will delete it.

9. Changes to this policy

If we make important changes, we will update the date above and tell you in the App before the changes take effect.
