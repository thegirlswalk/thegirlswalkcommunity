Support

Questions about the TGW Community app, an event, or your account? Get in touch.

Email: info@thegirlswalkcommunity.com
We usually reply within 2–3 business days.

Instagram: @thegirlswalkcommunity

When you email about the app, please include your phone model, the app version (shown at the bottom of the Me tab) and a screenshot if you can.

Safety concerns

If someone in the community makes you feel unsafe, use Report on their profile or post in the app, or email us with the subject "Safety". Reports are reviewed by a TGW admin as a priority. If you are in immediate danger, call 911.

Common questions

My steps aren't showing up

Check that the app has permission to read steps: on iPhone, go to Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices → TGW Community; on Android, open Health Connect → App permissions → TGW Community. Then open the app again. New steps can take a few minutes to appear.

I'm not getting notifications

Make sure notifications are turned on for TGW Community in your phone's settings, and that the notification types you want are on in the app under Me.

Who can see my steps?

By default, only members of your circles. You can change this in the app under Me → Step visibility.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, go to Me → Delete account, or follow the steps on our account deletion page.
