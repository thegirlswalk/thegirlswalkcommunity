Delete your account

This page explains how to delete your account in the TGW Community app, developed by TheGWalk Community, and what happens to your data.

Option 1: In the app

  1. Open the TGW Community app and sign in.
  2. Tap the Me tab.
  3. Scroll down and tap Delete account.
  4. Confirm. You will be signed out, and your account is deleted.

Option 2: By email

If you can't open the app, or you've already removed it:

  1. Email info@thegirlswalkcommunity.com from the email address on your account, with the subject "Delete my TGW account".
  2. We will reply within 5 business days to confirm the request is really from you.
  3. Once confirmed, we delete your account and data within 30 days.

DataWhat happens

What we keep, and for how long

  • Encrypted backups: copies are removed automatically within 60 days after deletion.
  • Safety reports: if you made or were named in a report about a safety concern, we may keep a minimal record (no step data) for up to 1 year to protect the community.
  • Donation records: if you donated, the payment provider and we may keep the records the law requires.
  • Anonymous totals: combined statistics such as "total community steps" don't identify you and are not deleted.

Only want to delete some data?

You don't have to delete your whole account:

  • Step data: email us with the subject "Delete my step data" and we'll erase your stored step history within 30 days. To stop new steps being shared, turn off access in Apple Health or Health Connect.
  • Profile photo or city: remove them in the app under Me.

More detail is in our Privacy Policy.
